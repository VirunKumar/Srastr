Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

Srastr Technologies ("we", "us", "our") operates Quantivo Pro. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

2. Data We Collect

2a. Account Data (Optional)

  • Name, email address or mobile number (for login only)
  • Country of residence (for currency formatting)

2b. Calculation Data

  • Health & Fitness (e.g. BMI, Calories) — processed 100% locally on your device. NEVER sent to our servers.
  • General History (e.g. EMI inputs) — stored entirely locally on your device. We do not sync this to our servers.

2c. Personal Insights

  • Which calculators you use and your activity streak
  • Basic calculation metrics (e.g. total EMI planned)
  • This data is used strictly to power your personal Insights dashboard and is NEVER sent to our servers.

2d. Payment Data

  • We do NOT store payment card details
  • Razorpay process payments; we only receive a payment confirmation ID

3. How We Use Data

  • To provide secure account access via OTP
  • To generate subscription tax invoices

4. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your data. We share minimal data with:

  • Resend: To send OTP codes (your email only)
  • Razorpay: To process payments (no card data stored by us)
  • Google AdMob: To serve ads to Free tier users. Google handles ad targeting and may collect device identifiers as per their policy. (Pro users see no ads).

5. Your Rights (GDPR / DPDP)

Under GDPR (EU) and DPDP (India), you have the right to:

  • Erasure: Delete your account via Settings → Delete Account
  • Correction: Update your profile in Settings
  • Consent Withdrawal: Change privacy preferences anytime in Settings

6. Health & Financial Data

All calculations, including highly sensitive ones (like Health & Fitness data) and general history, are processed and stored 100% locally on your device. They are NEVER sent to our servers.

7. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained until you delete your account
  • Calculation history: Stored locally on your device and retained until you clear it or uninstall the app.
  • Personal insights: Stored locally on your device (limited to 500 recent actions) and retained until you clear them or uninstall the app.

8. Contact Us

For any privacy-related concerns or inquiries, you can reach out to us:

  • support@srastr.com
  • support@srastr.com (GDPR/DPDP India)

Quantivo Pro is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
